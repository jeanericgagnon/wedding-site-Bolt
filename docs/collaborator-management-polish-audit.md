# Collaborator management polish audit

Date: 2026-04-13

## Current collaborator management strengths
- owner can create DB-backed invites
- owner can see invite list
- owner can copy/resend/revoke invites
- invite accept flow exists
- invite safety is much stronger now

## Current weakness
The area is still more of an invite management panel than a full collaborator management center.

### Missing polish
- no clear separation between active collaborators and pending invites
- no role-change action for accepted collaborators
- no remove-access action for accepted collaborators

## Honest conclusion
This is already **done enough for v1**, but one more usability pass could make it feel less provisional.

## Best next move
If we do one more thing, it should be:
- visually separate pending invites from active collaborators

Not a full role-edit system right now.

## Next step
- B3.2 decide whether that one separation pass is worth it or whether to stop here
