# RSVP Exception Cases

Date: 2026-04-13

## First explicit exception cases

### 1. Split household response
Example:
- two people in the same household do not answer the same way
- one attends, one declines

Why it matters:
- household grouping should not erase individual RSVP truth

### 2. Guest swap
Example:
- the invited guest cannot attend, but another person wants to come in their place

Why it matters:
- this should not be treated as a simple plus-one or a normal confirmed RSVP

### 3. Partial reply
Example:
- guest answers the ceremony but not the reception
- guest answers attendance but not meal choice
- guest answers for themselves but not their plus-one

Why it matters:
- “confirmed” can hide unresolved operational work if the product flattens the response too early

### 4. Unnamed plus-one
Example:
- guest confirms they are bringing someone but does not provide the actual person’s name

Why it matters:
- seat count and household truth may still be unresolved

### 5. Admin-overridden / manually handled response
Example:
- couple or planner records the RSVP after a phone call, text, or family relay

Why it matters:
- admin truth should stay distinct from guest-submitted truth

## Recommendation
These exception cases should become visible states in guest ops instead of staying buried in notes or implied by weird data combinations.
