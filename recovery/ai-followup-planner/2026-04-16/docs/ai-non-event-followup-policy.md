# AI Non-Event Follow-Up Policy

Use this to decide which single non-event clarification is worth asking in a round.

Goal:
- improve site coverage without wasting tokens
- choose the highest-leverage non-event question
- avoid repetitive story-only bias

## Non-event categories
### 1. guest-feel
Ask when:
- style words are generic or shallow
- the site needs emotional direction more than factual cleanup
- one answer will improve hero, intro, FAQ tone, travel tone, and schedule tone

Good for:
- thin baselines
- generic style words like `relaxed`, `modern`, `romantic`, `fun`

### 2. story-detail
Ask when:
- story is missing or too thin
- a single detail would noticeably improve story/hero warmth
- guest-feel is already clear enough

Good for:
- strong operational baseline but flat personal texture

### 3. location-why
Ask when:
- the location is known
- the setting seems meaningful or destination-like
- one answer could improve hero, travel, and story framing

Good for:
- destination weddings
- distinctive locations
- situations where guests will naturally wonder why this place matters

### 4. registry-posture
Ask when:
- registry intent is vague or not set
- gifts language will otherwise feel awkward or generic
- there is no more important emotional or operational clarification to ask first

Good for:
- travel-heavy weddings
- no-pressure gift language
- couples unsure whether to mention gifts at all

### 5. meeting-city
Ask when:
- story starts digitally / remotely
- city of first in-person meeting would sharpen the story materially
- it beats story-detail in leverage

Good for:
- online-origin couples
- long-distance beginnings

## Selection order
Use this order only after deciding a non-event question should exist in the round:
1. guest-feel
2. location-why
3. story-detail
4. meeting-city
5. registry-posture

## Token discipline
When live AI is involved:
- choose one non-event question max per round
- if the event lane already asked a broad high-leverage cluster, the non-event question must be worth it
- if the answer would only improve one small line, skip it
- if the answer improves several sections, it wins

## Skip rule
Do not ask a non-event follow-up if:
- the draft is already believable and strong enough
- the category is clearly undecided and not useful yet
- the question would just satisfy curiosity instead of improving the site
