# Clarifying Question Three Loops

## Loop 1
Observation: 0-1 question bias is too strict for building a truly strong site.
Adjustment: shift local target to 0-2 by default, max 3 when the site would otherwise be weak/confusing.
Lesson: asking nothing is still right for strong cases, but normal cases often need more than one follow-up.

## Loop 2
Observation: “ask anything ambiguous” is too broad and leads to endless cleanup.
Adjustment: only ask when the ambiguity/missing info would materially improve the final site.
Lesson: ambiguity alone is not enough; site impact is the filter.

## Loop 3
Observation: guest clarity and operational usefulness still beat trivia/story in most weak cases.
Adjustment: keep bias toward guest expectation, event understanding, and confusion reduction before detail-mining.
Lesson: the system should follow up on weak/ambiguous areas that improve site quality, then stop once the site is strong enough.
