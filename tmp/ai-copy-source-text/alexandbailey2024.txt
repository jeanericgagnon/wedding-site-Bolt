The ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception will all be held at the Club Lake Plantation Wedding Venue, located 30 minutes north of Orlando, FL. WHERE SHOULD I STAY? The bridal party and immediate family will be staying in the nearby town of Mount Dora, about 20 minutes from our venue. Mount Dora is a quaint little town with tons of shopping, lake-front restaurants, and activities to fill your downtime. For guests choosing to stay in the area, there is a Hampton Inn hotel and/or several rental options via Airbnb and Vrbo. Please let us know if you have any questions or need help finding a place to stay! HOW DO I GET THERE? WILL THERE BE PARKING? We are working on getting a transportation service to and from the wedding party's hotel in Mount Dora and our venue in Apopka. However, if you are not staying at/near the hotel, you can drive or schedule an Uber or Lyft . If you choose to drive, there is a large parking area for guests with parking attendants to assist. WHAT TIME SHOULD I ARRIVE? Our ceremony will begin promptly at 5:00pm. We ask that guests arrive 15-30 minutes prior to the ceremony start time. This will allow you plenty of time to park, grab a drink, and find a seat! WHAT SHOULD I WEAR? We would love to see our family and friends dressed up with us! We are requesting cocktail/semi-formal attire for our wedding. No denim or jeans please! CAN I BRING A GUEST/DATE? If you received a plus one or your guest's name is listed on your invitation, then yes! Otherwise, we would love to keep our wedding as an intimate event with close family and friends only. ARE CHILDREN INVITED? We love all the littles! However, invitations will be specific and we request you only bring children that are included on your invite. WILL THERE BE AN OPEN BAR? YES! The drinks are on us and the hangover is on you...get ready to party! CAN I TAKE & POST PICTURES OF YOUR WEDDING ON SOCIAL MEDIA? We will be having an "unplugged ceremony", meaning no phones or cameras. We have hired professionals to capture these precious moments for us and promise to share our photos as soon as we receive them! After the ceremony, feel free to take & post as many pictures/videos as you would like. Our only request is that you tag each photo with our wedding hashtag... #JohansonInTheMoonlight WHAT SHOULD I DO IF I CAN'T MAKE IT? Your presence will surely be missed. If you cannot attend, please let us know as soon as possible and RSVP "will NOT attend", that way we can plan accordingly. Thank you!
