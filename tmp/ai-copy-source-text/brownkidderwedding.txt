Andrew and Alex's mutual friend, Hannah, invited Alex to Baylor University for a visit. They hit it off, but the timing wasn't right and they fell out of touch for a few years. After Andrew graduated college, he traveled with his friend Dustin to go camping in Corpus Christi. Andrew contacted Hannah to grab a drink and catch up, and she brought Alex along. The timing was perfect and they fell in love. Six months later, Alex moved to Dallas to live with Andrew, and after three years, Andrew asked her to be his wife. Now they can't wait to spend the rest of their lives together.
